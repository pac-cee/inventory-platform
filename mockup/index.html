    About this mockup

    A UI-only preview of the planned upgrade to pac-cee/inventory-platform. Nothing here talks to an API; the rules (return ceiling, damage vs. loss, repair needs inventory management, payment approval is admin or cashier, MoMo mock mode) are re-implemented in the browser from docs/SPEC.md and src/lib/roles.ts.

    People, locations, the 28 catalogue items, stock, the ledger, payments and the 2,500,000 RWF budget come from the app's own mock fixtures (src/lib/api/mock/fixtures.json). Racks A–C and the parts on them are sample shelving. Departments, assignments and floor layouts are new in this mockup and are saved only on this device.

    Building shapes are placeholders until they are matched to site photos.
